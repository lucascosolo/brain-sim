# P2-E3 contract red-team (before commit)

Two fresh critics reviewed the draft contract (commit 'P2-E3 contract: DRAFT') before it was finalised. Recorded verbatim. Every must_fix and should_fix was adopted in the final contract; 'consider' items were adopted except where noted in review/ledger.jsonl.

## Critic: falsifiability

Verdict: Not ready to commit. Leakage is clean: seeds 11-15 appear in no review cache, and the forward store keeps its own streams. After the 50 s settle the continuation leaves vbar unchanged (seed 43: 3.69 mV with or without it). Exploratory runs on seeds 42-43 (excluded from P2-E3) show three problems. The new drift bar would void runs for reasons unrelated to the hypothesis. The 0.25 mV grid can step over the no-inhibition arm's window. Separate D1/D2 let up to 20 % of items carry wrong content.

- [must_fix] The bar |signed vbar drift| <= 0.1 mV over the last 10 s of the 50 s settle sits at the residual a 5-tau settle leaves, not at noise. Exploratory seed 43: -0.105 mV (with continuation) and -0.102 (without) at M=1,000, -0.119 at 1,500, -0.101 at 2,000; seed 42: -0.048; seed 0: -0.038. Runs would go INVALID for reasons unrelated to the hypothesis, and 'each test phase' includes the reported loads.
  - fix: Keep P2-E2's own rule unchanged at gated loads (mean |dvbar| <= 0.2 mV; seed 43 gives 0.113) and report signed drift. Or derive a signed bar in the contract from sensitivity: 5.5 mV of excess cost C1 0.065, so 0.1 mV costs about 0.001. Either way, M=1,500/2,000 are flagged, never invalidating. Keep the 50 s settle.
- [must_fix] The matched g=0 arm is not truly matched: the 0.25 mV grid can step over its window. Exploratory seed 42 (lag 1 ms, 75 ms window): at M=1,000, g=0 passes D1 and D2 (as separately gated) only near 1.4 mV (0.915 / 0.95). At 1.3 D1 fails (0.845); at 1.5 D2 fails (0.87). At M=250 it passes from 1.3 to 2.6. Grid points 1.25 and 1.50 both miss, so 'no window without inhibition' could be a grid artefact.
  - fix: Use a 0.05 mV step from 1.00 to 6.00 for both arms; one memory raster serves all arms, so it is cheap. Predeclare the comparison's reading: each arm's passing-window width on seed 0, and its joint score at M=1,000 per gated seed. Label the g=0 arm at the main J_fb* as an over-excitation check that cannot pass (seed 42: D2 0.185 at 2.5 mV).
- [must_fix] D1 and D2 are separate per-item fractions. Up to 10 % of items may lack their content and a different 10 % may carry intrusions, while the claim still reads '>= 90 % of items'. Seed 42, g=0 at 1.4 mV, M=1,000: D1 0.915, D2 0.95, joint 0.865. The draft's g=0 prediction cites a joint score (0.87) that the gate does not use.
  - fix: Gate D1 and D2 on the same item: >= 80 % of missing lines and < 10 intrusions, for >= 90 % of cued items. Apply this in calibration, on gated seeds, in D4 and in the matched comparison. Report D1 and D2 separately as well. This is stricter and is decided before commit, so it lowers no bar.
- [should_fix] g = 0.3 and the predicted window (J_fb* about 3, 2-4.5 mV) came from exploration with inhibition in the same tick as excitation and a -20 mV floor. The draft lags inhibition by 1 ms. Seed 42, M=1,000, 75 ms, D2 < 10: same-tick passes 2.5-4.0 mV, while the lagged version passes 2.0-3.0 (D2 0.88 at 3.5, 0.675 at 4.0). The floor changed nothing. g was never checked under the lag, so a FAIL could come from that interaction alone.
  - fix: Before commit, rerun the exploration on seeds 42-43 with the exact rec model (1 ms lag, no floor, 75 ms window, D2 < 10, all three loads), then rewrite the predictions. Keep g = 0.3 citing that run, or calibrate g on seed 0 over a small predeclared grid with a written selection rule. State 'no floor' and every rec constant explicitly.
- [should_fix] The shuffled-feedback void cannot fail. Each responder projects to about 13 % of lines (F density 0.133, seed 42), below g = 0.3, so the rec layer stays silent and D1 is about 0. Like P2-E2's V-B, it only detects an implementation leak. Nothing tests that the regenerated lines are the cued item's own rather than an artefact of store statistics.
  - fix: Keep it, as the owner requires, labelled 'fails by construction; leak check'. Add a reported label-permuted arm that writes R(x)'s feedback onto item pi(x)'s eligible lines; it keeps every per-cell and per-line degree. Report the chance level: another item's lines regenerated under x's cue. Add a plateau-set arm, feedback from A(x) instead of R(x), to show what the activity rule adds.
- [should_fix] The instructive term is the answer by design. The eligible set is about 98.7 % of item lines plus 0.58-0.62 background lines per item (P2-E1 records), so the rule stores the input verbatim. That is input-supervised hetero-association, not a teacher current, but the pairing is not coincident: the post term is a 200 ms encoding trace, the pre spikes fall in [200, 250) ms. A coincident >= 1 spike in 50 ms at 40 Hz would miss about 13.5 % of lines.
  - fix: Label it as a BTSP-like instructive eligibility trace with fixed 1:1 line-to-rec wiring. Require the code to derive it from encoding spike counts, never from item indices, and pin that with a unit test in which one item line is silenced. State that the background lines baked into the store (about 0.6 per item) score as intrusions.
- [should_fix] D5 does not depend on J_fb, yet the calibration pass rule requires it. Seed-0 memory C1 at M=1,000 is about 0.935 at J* (0.925 at 1.50, 0.945 at 1.55), so a memory slip would FAIL P2-E3 before any content test. On gated seeds, a failure of D5 alone would stop the content line for a P2-E2 replication reason.
  - fix: Calibrate J_fb on D1-D4 (joint) only, and report D5 at calibration. Keep D5 gated on seeds 11-15, but predeclare that a failure of D5 alone is recorded as a P2-E2 replication failure, with the content criteria still reported, not as evidence against content completion.
- [consider] 'Everything in P2-E2 is unchanged: ... learning' contradicts the continuation, which raises learning-time vbar. Seed 43 offset after learning: 9.67 vs 9.34 mV at M=1,000, 18.13 vs 17.62 at 2,000. After the settle the offsets are equal (3.69, 5.40/5.39, 6.96 mV). R(x) still forms at non-equilibrium learning-time thresholds, and engine.py gains a new projection type.
  - fix: Reword: the forward store and the test protocol are unchanged, and episodes now last 250 ms. Report the continuation-time vbar offset and Jaccard(R(x), A(x)) per load. Add a test that the modified engine reproduces a recorded P2-E2 point exactly (e.g. seed 6, M=250). Record a feedback-store digest per seed.
- [consider] The 75 ms window is generous next to the predicted 30-40 ms rec latency, and it moves the J_fb window: on seed 42 with the lag, 50 ms passes 2.5-3.5 mV and 75 ms passes 2.0-3.0. Two things go unscored at test: intrusions after 75 ms, and memory spikes in 50-75 ms, which C2 never sees but which drive the rec layer.
  - fix: Keep 75 ms as the gated window. For every arm, also report D1, D2 and the joint criterion at 50 ms, and intrusions over the whole 100 ms cue plus the 200 ms gap, to show the inhibition comparison does not depend on the window.
- [consider] The hypothesis says inhibition 'makes it specific', but the verdict ignores the matched arm, so that clause is never tested. D1 is also nearly all-or-none per item, since every eligible line of x gets identical feedback from R(x). It acts as a thresholded re-read of assembly recall, not a measure of graded content fidelity.
  - fix: Move the inhibition clause to a predeclared secondary claim, read by window width and joint score. Report the per-item distribution of the missing-half fraction and HD(r,x)/HD(x',x). Do not claim graded fidelity beyond what that distribution shows.
- [consider] The counting estimate's '2.8 inputs to a non-item line' uses a 14 % density that includes x's own lines. With the measured density of 0.133 at M=1,000 (seed 42), the non-item rate is (0.133*4000-100)/3900, about 11 %, or about 2.2 of 20. The error is conservative, but the arithmetic is wrong.
  - fix: Correct the arithmetic and cite the measured density. Base the predictions on an exploratory run of the exact readout (see the lag issue), labelled exploratory.

## Critic: implementation_risk

Verdict: Not implementable exactly as written yet. Two must-fixes. First, the new 0.1 mV signed-drift validity bar sits at measured values (0.10-0.12 mV at M >= 1,000 on seed 43, even with plain P2-E2 learning), so it would void runs for no scientific reason. Second, the contract leaves open whether learning continues from a tested state, and the feedback store depends on it. Arms, off-grid J*, HD, spontaneous spikes, streams and protocol scope need pinning. With the contract's dynamics, seed 42 has a g=0.3 window of 2.00-3.25 mV.

- [must_fix] The new validity bar (|signed mean dvbar over the last 10 s| <= 0.1 mV) sits at measured values. Scratch runs, not results: at M=1,000, -0.105 mV (seed 43, with continuation), -0.102 (seed 43, plain P2-E2 learning), -0.044/-0.048 (seed 42); -0.119 at M=1,500 and -0.100 at 2,000. Mean |dvbar| was 0.076-0.129, under P2-E2's 0.2. A run could go INVALID for no scientific reason.
  - fix: Keep P2-E2's rule and bar unchanged (mean |dvbar| <= 0.2 mV) as the validity gate and report the signed drift beside it, or set signed <= 0.2 mV. Say which loads it covers. P2-E2 applied it to every phase, and paired seed 5 went invalid at 2,000. Recommended: gate at 250/500/1,000, report at 1,500/2,000.
- [must_fix] The contract does not say whether learning continues from a tested state. BTSP's store is unaffected, but the feedback store depends on vbar through the continuation responders. Seed 43, scratch run: settling and testing at M=250, then learning to 500, changed the responders for 40 of items 251-500 and 4,021 of ~987k feedback synapses, against learning straight on. P2-E2's calibrate() tested copies; run_seed() tested main and kept learning.
  - fix: State that every test phase runs on a deep copy, and that learning always continues from the untested state, in calibration and gated runs alike. Record the feedback-store sha256 at each load, and add a validity or unit check that it equals a learning-only run's store at the same M.
- [should_fix] g=0.3 and the 2-4.5 mV window come from exploration with different dynamics: same-tick inhibition, a -20 mV floor on rec v, a rec reset at each cue, a 50 ms window and an intrusion bar of 25. Re-run with the contract's dynamics (exc 1 ms, inh 2 ms, no floor, continuous state, 75 ms, <10), seed 42, exploratory: g=0.3 passes 2.00-3.25 (M=1,000 D2 0.91 at 3.25, 0.85 at 3.5), so J* = 2.625; g=0 passes nowhere (best 1.50, D2 0.875).
  - fix: Say explicitly: rec v has no floor, and rec state is never reset within a test phase. Inhibition lags by 1 ms, so a cell can cross on excitation before inhibition lands, and the Willshaw description is approximate. Revise the predictions to a window of about 2-3.3 mV and J* about 2.6, and note that g was found with same-tick inhibition.
- [should_fix] '42 arms in one simulation' is cheap only if the arms share the feedback matrix. As engine Projections, each arm needs its own CSR: ~2.13M feedback synapses at M=1,000 x 20 B is ~43 MB per arm, 1.8 GB for 42, doubled under deepcopy. Global inhibition as synapses would be 320 MB per arm. Float32 sums of N copies of g*J can differ in the last ulp from g*J*N, so a gated arm on another code path may not reproduce seed 0.
  - fix: Specify one rec population of K x 4,000 cells. One shared feedback CSR gives integer per-line counts each tick, scaled by each arm's J (exact on the 0.125 grid). Inhibition is one scalar per tick into ring[(t+2)%3] of a d_max=2 ring. Use the same code for calibration (K=42) and gated runs. Unit-test it spike-for-spike against an engine LIF+Projection on a tiny net.
- [should_fix] J_fb* is the midpoint of a run, so with an even number of passing grid points it lies off the 0.25 grid (2.625 in the seed-42 check). No seed-0 numbers then exist at J_fb*, yet the Calibration result must record 'the seed-0 numbers at J_fb*' as item-level predictions. The matched g=0 arm's J and the g=0 arm at J_fb* have the same problem.
  - fix: Predeclare that after J_fb* is chosen, and before any gated seed, seed 0 is re-read once from the same streams, with extra arms at J_fb* (g=0.3 and g=0) and at the matched g=0 J. Those numbers are the predictions. Alternatively, round to the grid by a stated rule.
- [should_fix] 'Spontaneous rec spikes in the last 10 s of each settle' is zero by construction. Rec's only input is memory, and memory fired 0 spikes in the last 10 s of every settle checked (seeds 42 and 43, M 250-2,000). It is also undefined: per arm or pooled? A count or a rate? And does rec run during the settle?
  - fix: Define it: rec arms start at rest at the start of each settle, and each arm reports its rec spike count over the last 10,000 ticks, predicted 0. Add a measure that can be non-zero: per-arm rec spikes in the 200 ms after each cue offset (memory fires about 1 spike per gap) and outside [onset, onset+75).
- [should_fix] HD(r, x)/HD(x', x) is not defined precisely enough: is r the rec spikes in [onset, onset+75) alone, or r united with the cue? With r alone, unregenerated visible lines count as errors, and regenerating nothing scores 2.0. HD(x', x) is always 50. In the seed-42 check with r alone, the median is 0.04 at J 2-3, because the visible half is regenerated too.
  - fix: Define r = rec cells spiking in [onset, onset+75), x = the item's 100 lines (non-eligible ones included) and x' = the 50 cue lines, computed per item. Report the median and the 10th and 90th percentiles, optionally also for r united with x'. State that lines missed through eligibility count as errors.
- [should_fix] Continuation details are missing: the stream id and key (E1/E2 use ids 0-6 and 8); no quiet() between encoding and continuation; the first continuation tick integrates the last encoding tick's input through pre-write synapses; whether rec runs during learning. And 'everything in P2-E2 is unchanged' is not exact: the continuation shifts vbar (seed 43, M=1,000: offset after learning 9.66 against 9.34 mV), so memory activity is not bit-identical to P2-E2's.
  - fix: Name the stream, e.g. stream(seed, 9, k) for item k, which is robust to interleaving. State that there is no quiet(), that the first tick uses the pre-write input, that rec is not simulated during learning, and that the feedback CSR is loaded once per test phase. Reword: the forward store is unchanged; memory-layer activity differs slightly, and D5 measures it.
- [should_fix] 'The protocol is P2-E2's' leaves open what runs: the 60 s persistence phase (C5) at M=1,000, the fixed-threshold, random-store and repeated-cue arms, and the full-cue rec readout. D5 lists C1-C3 but not C4/C5. The loads for both g=0 arms and the shuffled arm are unstated, as are the loads validity covers. A reviewer could call any of these choices a deviation.
  - fix: List exactly what runs at each load: settle, then half, novel and full cues, with the main and both g=0 rec arms at every load, and the shuffled arm at M=1,000 only. Say whether the 60 s phase runs (recommended, with D1-D3 reported after it) and that P2-E2's V-A, V-B and repeated-cue arms are not rerun. State why C4 and C5 are not in D5.
- [should_fix] 'Targets drawn uniformly from its own stream' is ambiguous: one stream per cell, or a dedicated stream, and with which key? The null also fails by construction, as P2-E2's V-B did: a missing line keeps only ~13% of the responders' feedback, and the exploration gave D1 <= 0.015. Failing it shows little.
  - fix: Key it, e.g. stream(seed, 10, M). Draw each cell's targets without replacement from all 4,000 lines, and read it as an extra arm in the same simulation. Label it a by-construction null and predict D1 about 0. Consider a stronger structural null: an item-label-permuted feedback arm, in which x's responders write onto pi(x)'s lines.
- [consider] 'D1-D5 >= 0.90' compresses eight fractions: D1, D2, D3, D4 completion, D4 intrusions, C1, C2 and C3. The g=0 fallback ('largest minimum over D1-D4') has no tie rule. On seed 42 the g=0.3 passing range ran to the top of the grid (6.0) at M=250 and 500; had M=1,000 done the same, the grid edge would have set J_fb*.
  - fix: List the eight fractions. In the fallback, a tie goes to the lower J. If a passing run touches 1.00 or 6.00, record it as censored and still take the midpoint, or predeclare how the grid is extended.
- [consider] Onset, window ticks and latency are not pinned down. The earliest causal rec spike is at k=2 (input to memory 1 ms, memory to rec 1 ms), so spikes at k=0-1 are leftovers. 'Regeneration latency' is undefined: per line or per item? Also about 0.6 non-item lines per item are eligible from 0.5 Hz background (P(>=3 in 200 ms) x 3,900). They receive feedback, so they score as intrusions.
  - fix: Define onset as the first tick with cue rates and the window as k = 0..74. Define latency as the median over regenerated missing lines of the first-spike k, then the median over items. Mention the background-eligible lines in the D2 prediction.
- [consider] E1.learn_one keeps `eligible` local, so the driver must re-implement or wrap the encoding. The store-identity check is then meaningful only against unmodified E1 code. P2-E2 checked it only at M=1,000, and its calibration records digested a different config from its gated ones (P2-E2 review F7).
  - fix: State that p2_e1_btsp.py is not edited, and compare the forward store with unmodified E1 learning at every gated load. Digest one config (J_fb=None) in the calibration, seed and verdict records alike.
