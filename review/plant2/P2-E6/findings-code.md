## Initial findings

1. **P1 — Gated selection does not establish authorized full-size evidence.** `seed_guard` and `verdict` select the last screen for a seed, without requiring a full-size authorized attempt and its mandatory load lineage. `verdict` also accepts the special small-test source marker and can hide a prior invalid/failed attempt under a later row. Independently reproduced entirely in memory: five `full_size=False` screens for 111–115 with source `SMALL_INTEGRITY_TEST_ONLY`, each claiming a passing complete screen, produce gated `PASS`. Small-config `run_seed` bypasses the seed guard and originally permits the default journal and gated IDs, providing an accidental path to this contamination. Require full-size, exact-source, complete mandatory-load/attempt evidence; preserve prior gated invalidity and reject ambiguous repeated attempts. Test records must not open calibration/gates or count toward the final verdict.

2. **P2 — Required paired evidence is deferred until after optional transfer.** Paired recall/specificity/joint discordance summaries are calculated only after all F40 work. A resource stop before or during optional F40 therefore preserves the mandatory loads but leaves paired summaries empty; the prospective screen can still pass. Produce and retain these comparisons once each required matched pair is available, before optional transfer or any later resource interruption.

3. **P2 — Unexpected implementation exceptions are classified as partial resource work.** The broad exception handler places an unexpected `ValueError`, `KeyError`, or other implementation exception on the same path as resource exhaustion and cancellation. After sufficient rows exist, the screen can even remain passing while `interrupted` records an error. Unexpected execution defects must explicitly prevent a successful capability claim and opening later seeds. Reserve ordinary PARTIAL handling for resource stops/cancellation or genuinely absent work; record unexpected defects as INVALID or another blocking implementation-error status.

All three findings were sent to the parent; selection and paired-evidence blockers were sent directly to the implementer while review continued.

## Closure — 2026-10-12

All three initial findings are closed after narrow inspection of the fixes and an independent run of the final seven small tests: **7 passed in 0.67s**. The original findings and independent mechanism/raw-reconstruction checks above remain preserved.

1. Selection now rejects small/test-only screens, requires one full-size started/screen lineage for the same seed/source/attempt and mandatory loads, recomputes screens from load/sanity/paired records, and requires the appropriate recorded authorization stage. Calibration/gating consume verified passing feasibility attempts; committed portable evidence hashes are checked. Gate readiness is tied to those attempts, frozen predictions and exact source; final verdicts require that readiness and reject duplicate attempts or prior gated INVALID. Small runs require development seed 101 and explicit non-production output paths. The regression checks confirm test-only and aggregate-only records cannot open gates or become a gated PASS.
2. Paired discordance summaries are generated and appended immediately when each matched checkpoint becomes available, before optional F40. The driver supplies paired evidence to the screen, which marks absent mandatory comparison keys PARTIAL. A later resource stop therefore cannot discard already-produced mandatory paired records or silently treat their absence as complete evidence.
3. Unexpected exceptions now set the integrity-invalid flag; ResourceStop and KeyboardInterrupt alone retain ordinary incomplete handling. Interrupted records explicitly carry `integrity_defect`, and verified lineage rejects such a defect. The new regression invokes the real small driver with a synthetic RuntimeError and confirms INVALID while preserving started/interrupted/screen records.
