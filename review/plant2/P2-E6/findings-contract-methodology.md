# P2-E6 independent contract methodology review

Reviewed the 2026-10-12 draft before implementation, against AGENTS.md, P2-R1, and frozen E1/E3/E5 contracts and source. File review only; no simulations or external literature verification. The findings below concern the draft as received, not the owner's subsequently proposed corrections.

**Disposition: revise before freeze.** The causal history is admissible: it reads past sensory eligibility, not future items or evaluation labels. Assigned plateau keys, episode boundaries, a sparsity prior, and global normalization remain supplied structure, mostly acknowledged correctly. No hidden answer-key access is required by the proposed equation.

**Must fix before implementation**

1. **Make the verdict an explicit condition-by-arm matrix.** E6 lines 97–118 say every mandatory load/seed must meet C1–C4, but do not specify the arm. Applied literally to all mandatory arms, this demands that the known-failing F10 baseline pass. State that absolute bars apply to the candidate at both loads for F10 and independent inputs; comparator arms supply comparisons, and F40 is reported only. State whether each relative margin must hold separately on every seed. The sentence “Content does not decide index success” also conflicts with the independent-content regression veto. Specify that correlated content is diagnostic while independent content preservation is a regression requirement. Preserve inherited C4 as two marginal 90% bars; it does not require every oldest item, or 90% of oldest items jointly, to pass both.

2. **Bound the anti-suppression claim.** Fixed J and accommodation parameters do not fix effective retrieval thresholds: changed weights change background drive and vbar. C1 >=0.90 and joint >=0.85 exclude trivial silence, but allow lower own-cell recall alongside fewer false positives. Probability permutation matches an expected flip budget, not realized flips, additions, depression, density, or operating point. A candidate advantage demonstrates benefit from assigning probabilities by history; it does not by itself isolate better discrimination from an operating-point shift. Record paired recall gains/losses, own/spurious/total responder counts, thresholds, and realized potentiation/depression/density for each arm. Either add a predeclared F10 recall-preservation requirement or narrow the interpretation to better joint performance at frozen parameter settings, allowing suppression to contribute. Do not call the control density-matched.

3. **Fully specify control state and scheduling.** A column permutation of weights alone leaves trained accommodation state attached to the wrong cells. Define the permutation stream and permute all cell-specific dynamic state consistently, or score a globally permuted candidate raster against unchanged A; label that as a symmetry-based sanity check. Specify empty-store initialization and test timing. Never-trained C1=0 does not establish silence: it only establishes that no cue recalls >=80% of A. Require zero responders if silence is the intended check. Explicitly inherit the frozen half/novel/full sequence, phase keys, masks, settle, and nested 500→1000 learning timeline for all arms; test copies must not advance the learning timeline's random generators, neural state, H, or episode counter.

4. **Separate integrity defects from unfavorable neural outcomes.** Digest disagreement, incorrect probability budget, and failed uniform-wrapper equivalence are integrity invalidities. High shuffled-column C1 can instead arise from excessive nonspecific firing; it is not proof that the experiment malfunctioned. Report it as a failed sanity/capability condition unless an integrity defect is found. Likewise, retain the inherited settle-drift bar but expose nonconvergence as a measured protocol failure; do not silently discard or replace such seeds. State the disposition and rerun rule for integrity-invalid exploration/calibration/gate runs before observing them. E5's forward-equals-plain-E1 check must apply only to the uniform wrapper, never to the candidate: the changed forward store is the experiment.

5. **Make feasibility and resource stopping executable rules.** “Core index requirements” is ambiguous between absolute C1–C4/joint bars and the relative margins. Define the exact seed101/102 go/no-go vector, including the treatment of independent regression, comparator failure, invalidity, and missing data. Define required work after a failure: both F10 loads and comparators, independent regression, and whether F40 remains required. Fix an ordering that completes mandatory evidence before optional diagnostics. Give the 60-minute clock a precise start, specify whether running processes finish a load, and make the ceiling override any instruction to complete missing work. A failed feasibility screen and incomplete required diagnostics should both remain visible; neither is a five-seed verdict. No replacement seeds or post-result changes.

6. **Freeze an auditable implementation target.** The promised freeze entry is absent. Before implementation, record the final contract digest/commit, inherited source pins, exact stream definitions, and all verdict rules. Before gate execution, commit code hashes and feasibility results. Require append-only per-seed/load records plus portable per-cue index/content outcomes, provenance/digests, and sufficient retained stores or rasters to recompute the comparisons. R1 already requires this distinction between aggregate verification and raw replay. Declare the paired-interval method; family-dependent cues cannot supply 200 independent experimental replications.

**Limitations, not reasons to block this mechanism**

- The cumulative history estimates prevalence within supplied episodes; it does not learn representations, allocation, an online write trigger, or reconstructed teaching targets. The regular family schedule and stationary generator limit transfer claims. F40 helps but does not establish nonstationary robustness.
- C3's inherited novel cues are independent random items, not new exemplars from learned families. It tests that declared null population; it cannot establish rejection of related unseen experiences.
- Even exact realized flip-count matching per plateau cell would not match additions versus deletions or resulting density, because the two arms develop different stores. It strengthens the control without identifying a unique causal mediator.
- The original equation's denominator k−1+32 cancels under budget normalization. Rewriting it with H+0.8 is exact; changing to H+1 changes the prior slightly. A pseudocount of one is a defensible preimplementation revision, but must not be described as mathematically identical.
- The two-seed feasibility screen is a legitimate prospective resource rule. Its outcomes remain feasibility evidence, and selection through it must be disclosed beside any later five-seed result. No additional full-scale sweep is needed to resolve this review.

## Limited closure check, 2026-10-12

Checked only the six original findings against the revised preimplementation contract, including its decision-order appendix. No simulations. This closure does not itself freeze the contract.

1. **Scope: resolved in substance.** Candidate-only absolute bars, per-seed relative margins, separate oldest-item marginal bars, independent regression, and reported-only F40 are explicit. One terminology alignment remains: “gated index PASS requires ... every core/regression/control requirement” includes content regression, while the earlier paragraph distinguishes the combined experiment from the pure index verdict. Call the former **overall E6 PASS**, and report the pure index vector separately. This changes no numerical requirement.

2. **Suppression: resolved.** The added F10 C1 non-regression bar, paired response diagnostics, adaptive-state reporting, and narrowed causal interpretation address the concern. Exact per-cell realized flip matching is stronger than the original control; the contract correctly disclaims matching additions, depression, and density.

3. **Control state/schedule: resolved.** Raster permutation preserves total activity while disrupting identity. Its stream, fresh empty-store initialization, zero-responder check, inherited phase/cues, nested loads, and protection of main state/RNGs are specified.

4. **Integrity versus failure: resolved.** Outcome-dependent sanity failures and nonconvergence remain measured failures; integrity defects receive a distinct disposition. The uniform wrapper alone must reproduce the frozen forward store. Attempts persist, and gated seed replacement/rerun requires an owner ruling.

5. **Feasibility/resources: resolved with one checklist alignment.** The screen, ordering, partial-plus-failure reporting, clock start, grace period, and ceiling precedence are explicit. Add **F10 C1 >= baseline−0.03 at both loads** to the enumerated prospective screen vector: the paragraph immediately after that vector already requires it, but the vector currently names only absolute F10 bars and relative C2/joint margins. This prevents the machine-readable screen from accidentally omitting the new recall safeguard.

6. **Auditability: resolved at contract level.** The final publishing commit defines the freeze; inherited source pins, new-code hashes, append-only outcomes, portable per-cue data, and retained replay artifacts are required. Seed-level replication replaces the unspecified cue-level intervals. Actual compliance remains an implementation/results check.

**Disposition:** no further methodological review round is needed. Apply the two wording/checklist alignments above before publishing the binding freeze. The prior change from0.8 to1 is now correctly disclosed. The original limitations remain; none requires an additional mechanism or simulation before implementation.
