How evidence reconciles

What is allowed to change a command's state, and in what order.

  1. A result sets the command's acceptance or rejection. Nothing else can.
  2. stim_events set start and end when they are observed. Absence from stim_active sets nothing.
  3. A gap (sequence jump or tick discontinuity) marks every command that was accepted and not yet ended as "unknown since t = <last observed tick>", and the client requests stimlog.
  4. A stimlog entry with a matching stim_id fills whatever is unknown; the row then says "recovered from stimlog".
  5. If stimlog does not contain the stim_id — history expired, more than 64 newer stimuli, or a different run — the row reads "history expired; end never observed" and the timeline bar keeps its open "?" edge while it is in the window. Nothing is filled in.
  6. On reconnect within the same run, layout, config, status and stimlog are re-requested; the unobserved span is a gap and rules 3 to 5 apply.
  7. A result that arrives after the unconfirmed timeout replaces "unconfirmed". Nothing is ever resent automatically.

Rules

The equations the engine runs. Text, not a claim about outcomes.

  1. Membrane. Leaky integrate-and-fire with an adaptive threshold: the threshold rises after each spike and relaxes back, so a cell that has just fired needs more drive to fire again.
  2. Pair STDP, potentiation — at a post-synaptic spike:
    w += g · a₊ · x_pre · (w_max − w)
  3. Pair STDP, depression — at delivery:
    w ← max(w · (1 − g · a₋ · y_post), 0)
  4. Traces. x_pre and y_post gain +1 per spike and decay by exp(−1/20) per ms.
  5. Synaptic scaling. Once per sweep, wake only, on excitatory synapses whose presynaptic cell fired in that sweep:
    factor = 1 + clip(0.1 · (r_target − rate) / r_target, ±0.1)
