# Audit of the recovered repository (2026-10-10)

Done before any plant2 code. Four readers covered SPEC.md (sections 0-7, 8.0-8.24, 8.25-8.49),
`ui/stage*_results.json`, the plant code and the recovery notes, with an adversarial check of
the PASS/FAIL claims. The legacy suite was run here. Plant paths are frozen and were not
touched.

## What exists

- **The plant** (`brainsim/`, `server/`, `ui/`, `run.py`): 2,600 LIF cells with an adaptive
  threshold, dt 1 ms. Three regions: `sense` (200 E, a prosthetic nerve), `ctx` (1,600 E +
  400 I) and `hpc` (320 E + 80 I). Delta synapses with 1-8 ms delays. Plasticity: pair STDP
  scaled by a global gain, synaptic scaling, rate-driven structural growth and pruning, and a
  sleep flag. CPU-only, single-threaded NumPy.
- **The recovered `brainsim/` is a branch-line superset of master** (DECISIONS 2026-10-04). It
  carries flags for later experiments: `PENDING_STDP`, `PENDING_EXPRESS`, `HETERO_*` and the
  `gen2` profile. Master's byte-exact copies are in `docs/recovery/variants/master-f9411ab3/`.
- **The record**: SPEC 7 (Stage 0 kill tests K0.1-K0.13), SPEC 8.0-8.49 (Stage 1, about 50
  predeclared rounds). `ui/stage1_results.json` stops at 8.47.

## What the record proved and failed

| claim | status in the record |
|---|---|
| Stage 0 plant | **Not complete by its own record.** K0.1 (event cost) and K0.4 (immature pruning) are red. K0.2 and K0.5-K0.11 are green. The K0.3 population contrast was retired red (0.861 against 1.2). |
| Intrinsic homeostasis (K0.14: a rate-setpoint threshold offset replacing synaptic scaling) | Recommended 2026-09-12. An intrinsic-homeostasis branch existed and stayed unmerged (SPEC 970, 1253). The owner's closed gates list "rejected intrinsic homeostasis as previously run" (`docs/recovery/project-facts-2026-10-03.md`). |
| Use-dependent potentiation (S1.0) | **Rejected** under triplet STDP (1.029) and gated STDP (1.020); closed by the owner. K0.13: repetition habituates (ratio 0.922, evoked response 0.832 -> 0.428). |
| K1.1 one-shot binding and half-cue completion | **FAIL throughout.** Master: 1/16 recalled (8.2). Ten stacked proxies: best 5/16 (8.3-8.12). The pending tag's recall (8.30) turned out to be learned disinhibition (8.35-8.37). Excitatory binding (8.46): gain +2.4 to +7.0 cells over the never-trained twin on six seeds, but official K1.1 fails on assembly size (40-61 cells against 20; 8.47). |
| Persistence | **Lost.** The 8.46 gain halves within 10 s and then holds (8.48). Scaling is the largest single cause (51-87 %), and the processes compound (8.49). |
| K1.2-K1.5 (consolidation, replay, forgetting, engram survival) | Never run. They were blocked behind K1.1. |

**Why, in the record's own measurements:**

- Pair STDP's fixed point (0.45 `w_max`) sits below the homeostatic rest weight (0.6-0.9), so
  co-activity depresses the used pathway.
- The seconds-scale homeostat treats a stimulus as a rate error and erases writes. Scaling and
  structural elimination back each other up.
- The cortical code is dense, so local rules separate inputs only by a rate ratio of about 2.
- The wiring caps recall even with oracle weights (8.23).
- Readouts were often within a few cells of a never-trained twin, so that twin is a required
  control.

## What is broken or missing

- **Branch code.** Most branch code for 8.0, 8.1, 8.3, 8.13, 8.14, 8.17 and 8.25-8.49 was not
  recovered: drivers `k829`-`k849` and about 50 unit tests are missing. The best line (8.46) and
  the persistence diagnosis (8.49) cannot be re-run from this tree.
- **Logs.** Every log under `~/.cache/scratch/brainsim-*` is on the storage VPS, not here.
- **Legacy suite.** Measured here (Python 3.13.16, numpy 2.5.3, 18 min 40 s): 314 passed, 89
  failed, 1 skipped, 1 deselected. The failures are:
  - orphaned branch tests whose code is missing, the bulk of the 89 (`test_k11_window_on_w`
    17, `test_s10_gated` 14, `test_k11_restricted_donors` 11, `test_inh_homeostat` 10,
    `test_k11_prune_off` 7, `test_scaling_hold` 6, `test_k11_encode_window` 6,
    `test_k11_sparse_write` 5, `test_k03_instrument` 3);
  - recorded reds (K0.1, K0.4, K0.13, K1.1, K1.1 encode-mode);
  - bit-identity digest and replay pins (`test_engine_determinism`, `test_hetero_write` x2,
    `test_triplet`, `test_gen2_profile` and replay-equality checks inside others). These
    account for the 12 failures beyond the 77 recorded on 2026-10-04. They come from numpy's
    AVX-512 (X86_V4) kernels on this CPU, not from a code change or the numpy version. With
    `NPY_DISABLE_CPU_FEATURES=X86_V4`, `test_engine_determinism` passes 3/3 here (1 failure
    without it). The code-vs-record reader reports 12 of the 13 affected tests passing that
    way, and the same wrong digest under Python 3.11 with numpy 2.4.6.
  - `AGENTS.md`'s "120 known failures" is the 2026-10-03 count; the reconciliation recorded 77.
- **Record inconsistencies** (as found, not resolved):
  - the `ctx->hpc` in-degree is 30 in `params.py` but 40 in 8.32-8.33;
  - `stage1_results.json` labels the negative diagnostics 8.20 and 8.22B "pass", meaning
    valid;
  - SPEC section 4's synapse counts disagree with `S_MAX`.
- **Known engine defect.** A delay-ring slot-reuse defect is real but has not fired (8.15-D3).

## Consequence

The plant line cannot reach Stage 1 without changing its plasticity and homeostasis
together. The code of its best attempt is gone. plant2 starts beside it
(`DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-10; `docs/plant2/STAGES.md`).
